Where this comes from

Every event on this page was read from one of the sources below and links back to it. They are grouped the way the Church in Singapore is organised. Where an organisation publishes in more than one place, its links are listed together.

What's filtered: the Language menu starts at English only — switch it to All languages or Vernacular only to see events in Mandarin, Tamil and others. The Frequency menu starts with daily and weekly activities excluded. The regular liturgical schedule is left out altogether — Sunday and weekday Masses, weekly novena devotions and standing Rosary or adoration times. Check the parish's own page for those.

Know of something not listed? If an event or an organisation is missing — or if you spot a date that has changed — write to catholiceventssg@gmail.com with a link to wherever it is announced.

How to use this

Click any column heading to sort by it — click again to reverse.
Click a venue to open it in Google Maps.
Search matches event names, organisers, venues and who may attend.
Many events are more than one kind — a Feast Day Mass is both. The filled tag is the main kind; outlined tags are the others, and filtering by any of them will find the event.

About the data

Always confirm on the source page before attending. Dates, times and venues change, and this page is a snapshot taken on the date shown above.
Every event links back to the page it came from.
Details marked “See registration” were not published on the source page.
Not listed: regular Masses, novenas and standing devotions.
Use the Language and Frequency menus to widen the view — both start narrowed.
Best viewed on a laptop or desktop. The table view puts every detail side by side; on a phone the page falls back to cards and the filters fold behind a single button.

Tell us about an event

Something missing, or a date that has changed? Write to catholiceventssg@gmail.com.
The most useful message names the event, the date and time, where it is held, and a link to wherever it is announced — a parish page, a bulletin or a notice. Corrections are just as welcome as additions.
This is a volunteer project, so replies may take a little while.
