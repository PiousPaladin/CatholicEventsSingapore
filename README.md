# Catholic Events Singapore

An unofficial community calendar of Catholic events in Singapore — retreats, talks, feast days,
formation courses and family programmes, gathered from parishes, archdiocesan offices, Caritas
members and religious orders.

**Open the calendar: https://piouspaladin.github.io/CatholicEventsSingapore/**

Compiled from publicly available sources by a member of the public. This is not an official
publication of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Singapore or of any parish or organisation
listed. Details change, so always confirm on the event's linked source page before making plans.

## Privacy

The site counts visits without cookies: how many people come, roughly from where, on what kind
of device, and which organisers draw interest. It never records searches or which listing
anyone reads.

## Feedback

Know of an event that is missing, or a date that has changed? Write to
**catholiceventssg@gmail.com** with a link to wherever it is announced. Corrections are as
welcome as additions. The address is also in the page's footer and in its "Sources of Events"
panel.
